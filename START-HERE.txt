KN AESTHETICS STUDIO WEBSITE: START HERE
================================

SEE THE SITE
Unzip this folder first, then double-click index.html.

ADD YOUR DETAILS
Open site-settings.js in Notepad (Windows) or TextEdit (Mac).
Fill in your phone, WhatsApp number, email and address, then check:
  - opening hours
  - services and prices (the prices in there are samples)
  - the About story and Kim's bio
  - the Questions page policies
Save the file and refresh the website.
Yellow "Add your..." reminders disappear as you fill things in.

ADD YOUR PHOTOS
Put photos in these folders using these exact names:

  Home page mirror     images/site/hero.jpg
  About page           images/site/about.jpg
  Kim's photo          images/team/kim.jpg
  Gallery              images/gallery/photo-01.jpg up to photo-12.jpg

Names must match exactly, including lowercase and .jpg
Each gallery photo's caption and category is in site-settings.js.
For more than 12, add a line there and use photo-13.jpg, and so on.
Once one gallery photo is in, empty spaces hide automatically.
Tip: keep photos under about 500 KB so the site loads quickly on mobile data.

HOW BOOKINGS WORK
The Book and Contact forms open WhatsApp or email with the message
already written. The client presses send and it comes straight to you.
Nothing is saved on the website, and there's no calendar: you confirm
each booking yourself.

PUT IT ONLINE (FREE OPTIONS)
Easiest: go to app.netlify.com/drop, sign up, and drag this whole folder
onto the page. You get a working web address straight away.
Cloudflare Pages and GitHub Pages also work.
To use your own name, such as knaesthetics.co.za, buy the domain from a
registrar and connect it in your host's settings.
To update the site later, edit your files and upload the folder again.
